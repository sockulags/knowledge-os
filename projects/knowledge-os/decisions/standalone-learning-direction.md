## Decision

Knowledge OS continues as a standalone project, built for learning, with its own knowledge base. The `openknowledge-pivot` draft decision is withdrawn; there is no pending pivot onto OpenKnowledge or the Open Knowledge Format.

## Rationale

The value for the owner is practising decisions and working with a knowledge service, with implementation delegated to agents. Competing with OpenKnowledge on storage, editing, search, linking, agent integration, or Git collaboration is not the goal.

## Consequences

The reader and core may keep evolving on their own merits. The adapter boundary already built into the reader stays as good design, not as a hedge toward a pivot that is no longer planned.
