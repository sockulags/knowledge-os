## Decision

Pause new feature development of Knowledge OS as a standalone knowledge platform. Evaluate pivoting it into a narrow governance layer on top of OpenKnowledge and Open Knowledge Format instead of competing on storage, editing, search, linking, agent integration, or Git collaboration.

## Preserve for evaluation

The evaluation should test whether approval-gated conversational capture, explicit source and discovery trust boundaries, discovery review and promotion, and deterministic project-scoped context export remain useful as a smaller plugin or set of skills.

Do not merge or extend the standalone product direction until that comparison identifies a material capability that OpenKnowledge and OKF do not already provide.
